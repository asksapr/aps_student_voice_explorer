Interpretation and responsible use. Percentages are approximate shares of substantive open responses within each school and grade group, not percentages of all enrolled students. Themes may overlap; percentages do not sum to 100%. Grades 3–5 and grades 6–12 were coded using separate approaches, so cross-group percentages should not be treated as directly comparable. Very small counts and selected quotes require care: do not use quotations to identify students, and avoid public sharing of potentially identifying or sensitive responses. Actions are practical suggestions, not evaluated interventions. Source: APS Skills, Habits, and Mindsets Survey school-level analysis workbooks.
